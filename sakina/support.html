We’re here to help · نحن هنا للمساعدة

Contact 1ahmedrefaat@gmail.com. Include your device, OS, language, app version and the steps that caused the issue. Leave private reflections and location details out of screenshots.

Prayer times

Confirm your city, time zone, calculation method, Asr and minute offsets in Settings. Match your local mosque’s settings. Automatic location is optional and needs permission and internet for region lookup.

Counting

Touch counts one sujud; two advance one rak‘ah. Pause or undo an accidental count. Camera/proximity are experimental and need practice. Automatic wrist recognition is not implemented.

Reminders

Enable native notifications and check sound, Focus, volume and battery restrictions. Makkah and Madinah alarms are short excerpts. Web previews do not deliver native background alarms.

Data and privacy

Export a backup before changing devices. Settings → Delete local data clears this installation; exported files and separate watch/browser data remain separate. There is no Sakina cloud account or cloud recovery service. See our privacy policy.

Developer support payments

Support checkout is not live in the testing preview. If enabled in a future store build, the store will show its actual local price and handle payment. Support does not unlock prayer features.
